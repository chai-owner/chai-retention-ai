ChAi is designed for a short daily check, not an afternoon of dashboard-diving.

## Start with Today

**Today** is your morning brief. It shows the headline, accounts that need attention, what moved since yesterday, and a ranked list of actions.

## Then open Customers

The **Customer Risk Center** lists every customer with a health score, churn probability and revenue at risk. Sort by risk, or filter to a segment, then open anyone you want the full story on.

Each customer page shows:

- Why this customer is at risk, with the weight of each factor
- Recommended actions, with difficulty, impact and estimated revenue saved
- Score history, so you can see whether things are improving

## Use Planner for the bigger picture

**Planner** shows the metrics ChAi tracks for your business, your segment averages and where you sit against them. Use it when you are deciding what to fix across the whole book, not just one account.
