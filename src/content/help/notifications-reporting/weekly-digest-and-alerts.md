## The Monday digest

Every Monday morning ChAi emails a short summary: score movement across your book, accounts that slipped into a worse band, revenue at risk, and the top actions for the week.

## Daily brief

The Today screen is refreshed every night, so your brief is waiting when you sign in. There is no need to run anything manually.

## Changing recipients

Digest emails go to the members of your organisation. Add or remove people on the **Team** page — new members start receiving the digest from the next send.

## Exporting

Customer, transaction and support tables can be exported for reporting from their respective pages.
