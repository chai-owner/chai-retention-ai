## Inviting someone

1. Open **Team** in the left navigation.
2. Select **Invite member** and enter their work email.
3. Choose a role and send. They get an email invitation link.

## Roles

- **Owner** — full access, including billing and deleting the organisation
- **Admin** — full app access and team management, no billing
- **Member** — sees customers, scores and recommendations

## Seats

Each plan includes a number of seats. If you invite past your limit, ChAi tells you before sending and offers an upgrade. Removing a member frees their seat immediately.

## Leaving or removing

Owners can remove any member from the Team page. Removing someone revokes their access straight away; the data they added stays with the organisation.
