Ask ChAi answers questions using your own customer data — no dashboard-diving required.

## Good questions to start with

- How do I improve retention this month?
- Which customers should I call today, and why?
- What is driving churn in my lowest-scoring segment?
- Which accounts are worth the most if I save them?

## Tips

- Be specific about the timeframe or segment when you have one in mind.
- Ask follow-up questions — the conversation keeps its context.
- If the answer looks thin, check the data confidence strip at the top of the widget. Limited or stale data means the answer is limited too.

> Ask ChAi never invents numbers. If it does not have the data to answer, it will say so and tell you what is missing.
