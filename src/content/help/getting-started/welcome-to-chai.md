ChAi is your AI retention analyst. It watches how your customers behave, scores their health, predicts who is likely to leave, and tells you what to do about it in plain English.

## What you need

- A way to get customer data in — either a connected tool (Xero, HubSpot, Zendesk and others) or a spreadsheet upload
- About 15 minutes for onboarding, where ChAi learns how your business works

## Your first week

1. **Onboard.** Answer a few questions about your business so ChAi can pick the metrics that matter to you.
2. **Add data.** Connect an integration or drop in a CSV.
3. **Book your onboarding call.** We walk through your first assessment with you.
4. **Open Today.** Every morning you get a short brief: who needs attention, what changed, and what to do next.

> Scores get sharper as more history arrives. The first full picture usually appears within a few days of your first upload.
