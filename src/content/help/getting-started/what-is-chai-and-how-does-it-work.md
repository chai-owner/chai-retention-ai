ChAi is a customer retention analytics platform that helps recurring revenue businesses — gyms, agencies, insurance brokers, subscription services, and professional services — see who's about to leave, and exactly what to do about it.

Instead of guessing which customers are unhappy, ChAi connects to the data you already have and turns it into a clear risk picture. For each customer, ChAi shows:

- A churn probability — how likely this customer is to leave
- A risk breakdown — the specific reasons behind that score, so you understand why, not just that, a customer is at risk
- Recommended next steps, so you know where to focus first

ChAi is built to work with the data you have, even if it's incomplete or messy. You don't need clean, perfectly structured data to get started. See our Data Drop feature.
