ChAi works with the data you already have, but more (and more current) data generally means more accurate, better-supported risk scores. Useful data includes:

- **Customer activity or engagement data** (e.g. usage, attendance, interactions)
- **Payment and billing data** (e.g. payment status, failures, renewal dates)
- **Support interaction data** (e.g. tickets, response patterns)

You don't need all of these connected to get started — ChAi will generate scores from whatever's available, and scores improve in confidence as more data comes in. But remember, the **better your data is, the better your insights will be**.
