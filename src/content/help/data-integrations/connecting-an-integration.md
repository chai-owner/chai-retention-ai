ChAi currently connects with **HubSpot, Xero, Intercom, Freshdesk, FreshBooks, QuickBooks Online, and Zoho CRM**. All of them follow the same connection process:

1. Go to your **Integrations** settings in ChAi.
2. Select the integration you want to connect and click **Connect**.
3. You'll be redirected to that platform to log in and authorize ChAi's access.
4. Once authorized, you'll be returned to ChAi, and your data will begin syncing.

Once connected, data feeds into your customers' risk scores automatically — there's nothing further to configure unless you're troubleshooting a sync issue (see below).

> Looking for a specific integration and don't see it listed? Some integrations, like Zendesk and Salesforce, are in progress — reach out to support@askchai.tech to check status.
