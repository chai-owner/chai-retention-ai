Not every business has clean, well-structured data ready to connect through an integration — and that's fine. **Data Drop** is built for exactly that.

With Data Drop, you can upload data as you have it, without needing to reformat, clean, or restructure it first. ChAi is built to work with messy, real-world data rather than requiring a perfect spreadsheet before it can help you.

To use Data Drop:

1. Go to **Data Drop** in ChAi.
2. Upload your files — you can upload more than one.
3. ChAi will process what you've provided and begin incorporating it into your risk scoring.

If you're unsure whether your data is usable, upload it anyway — Data Drop is designed to handle imperfect data, not just pristine exports.
