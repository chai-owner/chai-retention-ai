## Your trial

Every new account starts on a 14-day Standard trial with full access. We email you before it ends. If you do nothing, access pauses until you choose a plan — your data stays safe.

## Plans

- **Core** — for small books of business, essential scoring and Today
- **Standard** — adds integrations, Ask ChAi and the weekly digest
- **Enterprise** — higher limits, more seats and priority support

## Changing plan

Go to **Settings → Billing**. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at your next renewal so you keep what you paid for.

## Payments and invoices

Payments are handled by Paddle, our merchant of record. Invoices and receipts come from Paddle and are also available from your billing page. Refunds follow Paddle's 14-day policy.

Questions about a charge? Email support@askchai.tech.
