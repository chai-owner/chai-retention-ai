## My dashboard is empty

Scores need data. Check the **Data** page — if no integration is connected and nothing has been uploaded, there is nothing to score yet. After your first upload, scores appear on the next nightly run.

## The numbers look wrong

Open **Data Quality**. It shows coverage and freshness for each source. Most surprises come from a stale sync or a column mapped to the wrong field, both of which you can fix there.

## A customer appears twice

Go to **Identity** and confirm or split the suggested match. ChAi merges the history once you confirm.

## An integration disconnected

Tools expire their access periodically. Reconnect from the **Data** page — nothing is lost, the next sync fills the gap.

## I can't sign in

Use the forgot-password link on the sign-in page. If the reset email does not arrive within a few minutes, check spam, then email support@askchai.tech.

## Still stuck?

Email **support@askchai.tech** and tell us what you were doing when it went wrong.
